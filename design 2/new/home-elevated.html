高級感的參考 What elevated looks like

從 Awwwards 的每日網站、攝影、旅遊三個分類看了 26 個，留下六個新的，加上你上次喜歡的三個。每個附上第一個畫面和往下捲一點的樣子，點圖片會打開那個網站。按喜歡或不是，寫下你喜歡的那一點（例如：照片好大、字很小、很慢、很空）。新首頁會從這些優點來做，不照抄。

From Awwwards' Sites of the Day, Photography and Travel lists, 26 looked at: six new ones kept, plus the three you liked last time. Each shows its first screen and a little further down; the pictures open the site. Mark it, and note the one thing you like (the huge photograph, the tiny type, the slowness, the emptiness). The new home page is built from those qualities, never copied.

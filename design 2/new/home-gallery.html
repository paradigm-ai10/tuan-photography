照片進場的方式 How the photographs arrive

素描變成影片不做了。這裡是 14 個網站的開場：照片散開、從旁邊飛進來、排成一排，或最後落在一整個畫面上。每個都錄了開場，會自己重複播放，名字可以打開真的網站。你說首頁最後還是要落在巴塞隆納的影片上，所以看的時候想像：最後那一張就是你的影片。按喜歡或不是，寫下喜歡的那一點。

The sketch turning into the film is dropped. Here are fourteen openings where photographs scatter, fly in from the sides, line up in rows, or end on one full screen. Each has a recording of its opening that loops; the name opens the real site. You want the home page still to land on the Barcelona film, so as you watch, imagine the last picture is your film. Mark each one and note the one thing you like.

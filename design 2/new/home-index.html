把「地方的索引」做得高級 The index of places, made high-end

你說喜歡「地方的索引」這個想法，但我做的那一版看起來太平、太普通。第一批七個你都沒感覺，所以上面加了第二批十個：四個風景攝影師、六個高級旅行網站，照片都大得多。每個兩張截圖，點圖片會打開那個網站。按喜歡或不是，寫下你喜歡的那一點；下一輪從你喜歡的幾點來做，不照抄。

You liked the idea of the index of places, but my version looked plain and flat. None of the first seven caught your eye, so a second set of ten is now on top: four landscape photographers and six high-end travel sites, all with much larger photographs. Two stills each, and the pictures open the site. Mark each and note the one thing you like; the next round is built from those, never copied.

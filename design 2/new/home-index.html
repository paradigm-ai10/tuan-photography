把「地方的索引」做得高級 The index of places, made high-end

你說喜歡「地方的索引」這個想法，但我做的那一版看起來太平、太普通。這裡是七個用「一列名字配照片」或很講究排版的網站，每個兩張截圖，點圖片會打開那個網站。按喜歡或不是，寫下你喜歡的那一點；下一輪從你喜歡的幾點來做，不照抄。

You liked the idea of the index of places, but my version looked plain and flat. Here are seven sites built on a list of names with photographs, or on very careful type; two stills each, and the pictures open the site. Mark each and note the one thing you like; the next round is built from those, never copied.

在 Awwwards 挑的六個有「高級感」的網站。每個點開，看第一個畫面再往下捲一點，按喜歡或不是，寫下讓你覺得高級的那一點（顏色、字、動的方式、節奏）。地球的轉場會從你選的那幾個的優點來做，不照抄。

Six high-end sites picked from Awwwards. Open each, look at the first screen and a little scroll, mark it, and note the one thing that feels high-end to you (colour, type, how things move, pace). The globe change is then built from those qualities, never copied.

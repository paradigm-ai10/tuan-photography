Elevation for the four sculptures (Matterhorn, Mount Fuji, Aoraki / Mount Cook, Sa Pa), cut from
Mapzen Terrain Tiles (terrarium, AWS Open Data, s3://elevation-tiles-prod), downloaded 2026-11-02
with his permission and reduced to 256 x 256 heights (<name>.bin: little-endian uint16, scaled
between meta.json min and max, in metres).
Data sources and attribution: https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md
(SRTM, GMTED, ETOPO1, NED, Japan GSI, Land Information New Zealand, and others).
